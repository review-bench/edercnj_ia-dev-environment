## 1. Contexto

Migra skills remanescentes em `targets/claude/skills/core/{release,epic,ops}/`:

- `release/`: `x-release`, `x-release-changelog` (já em `dev/` parcialmente — verificar duplicação) (~2).
- `epic/`: skills de gestão de épico que possam estar fora de `dev/` (~0-2).
- `ops/`: `x-ops-incident`, `x-ops-troubleshoot`, `x-status-reconcile`, `x-mcp-recommend`, `x-setup-env`, `x-doc-generate`, `x-arch-update`, `x-perf-profile`, `x-telemetry-analyze`, `x-telemetry-trend`, `x-spec-drift`, `x-adr-generate`, `x-ci-generate`, `x-security-pipeline`, `x-security-dashboard`, `x-runtime-eval`, `x-hardening-eval`, `x-owasp-scan`, `x-supply-chain-audit`, `x-dependency-audit`, `x-threat-model` (~21).

Total ~23-25 skills. Algumas com `requires-capabilities` específicas (e.g., `x-runtime-eval` requires `infra.runtime`, `x-security-pipeline` requires `infra.cicd`).
